### 2. How will Google read and index the first 20 pages?

When Google’s "Spider" (crawler) hits your site, it doesn't just look at text; it looks at **Structure** and **Metadata.**

* **The Sitemap Signal:** The `sitemap.xml` we just added acts as a map. Google reads this first to understand the scale of the site.
* **Semantic Understanding (JSON-LD):** Because we "stitched" FAQ Schema into every page, Google immediately sees your pages as "helpful answers" rather than just "sales pitches." This often leads to **Rich Snippets** (where your FAQs appear directly in the search results), which drastically increases your click-through rate.
* **Topical Authority:** By launching 15-20 pages at once that all link to each other, you avoid the "Thin Content" trap. Google sees a network of related information, which builds trust much faster than a single blog post would.
